# AnyCompany Commercial Burger Grill Model BG-450
## Product Manual & Operator Guide

### Product Overview
- **Model**: BG-450 Commercial Flat-Top Grill
- **Target Temperature**: 450°F (232°C)
- **Operating Range**: 200°F - 550°F (93°C - 288°C)
- **Cooking Surface**: 36 inches x 24 inches (864 sq inches)
- **Heat Source**: Natural gas or propane (convertible)
- **BTU Rating**: 90,000 BTU/hr
- **Dimensions**: 36in x 32in x 36in (W x D x H)

### Daily Operation

#### Startup Procedure
1. Verify gas supply valve is open
2. Turn main power switch to ON
3. Set thermostat to desired temperature (450°F for burgers)
4. Allow 15-20 minutes preheat time
5. Test surface temperature with infrared thermometer before cooking
6. Season cooking surface with thin layer of high-heat oil

#### During Service
- **Burger Cooking**: Place patties on hottest zone (rear). Cook 4-5 minutes per side for 1/4 lb patties.
- **Temperature Zones**: Rear of grill is hottest. Use front area for holding/warming.
- **Grease Management**: Push grease toward grease trough regularly. Empty grease trap when half full.
- **Surface Cleaning**: Scrape surface with grill brick between batches. Never use water on hot surface.

#### Shutdown Procedure
1. Turn thermostat to OFF
2. Turn gas supply valve to OFF
3. Allow surface to cool to below 200°F
4. Clean surface with grill brick and scraper
5. Apply thin coat of oil to prevent rust
6. Wipe exterior with damp cloth
7. Empty and clean grease trap

### Temperature Alerts
| Condition | Temperature | Action Required |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| Normal | 425°F - 475°F | Optimal for burgers |
| Low | Below 400°F | Check gas supply. Burner may need cleaning. |
| High | Above 500°F | Reduce thermostat. Check thermostat calibration. |
| Critical High | Above 550°F | Turn off immediately. Possible thermostat failure. |

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Symptom | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Won't ignite | No gas supply | Check gas valve is open. Check gas line. |
| Won't ignite | Igniter failure | Use manual lighter. Schedule igniter replacement. |
| Uneven heating | Clogged burner ports | Clean burner ports with wire brush (when cool) |
| Uneven heating | Warped cooking surface | Surface may need professional leveling or replacement |
| Temperature won't reach target | Low gas pressure | Contact gas supplier to check pressure |
| Temperature won't reach target | Dirty burners | Clean burner assembly |
| Excessive smoke | Grease buildup | Deep clean cooking surface and grease channels |
| Grease fire | Grease trap overflow | NEVER use water. Use fire extinguisher (Class K). Smother with metal lid. |
| Gas smell when off | Gas leak | Evacuate area. Call gas company immediately. Do NOT operate any switches. |

### Safety Information
- Keep Class K fire extinguisher within 10 feet of grill at all times
- Never leave operating grill unattended
- Wear heat-resistant gloves when cleaning hot surfaces
- Ensure kitchen hood ventilation is operating before igniting grill
- Train all operators on fire suppression procedures
