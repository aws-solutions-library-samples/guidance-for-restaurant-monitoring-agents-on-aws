# AnyCompany Walk-In Cooler Model WC-3800
## Product Manual & Operator Guide

### Product Overview
- **Model**: WC-3800 Commercial Walk-In Cooler
- **Target Temperature**: 38°F (3.3°C)
- **Operating Range**: 33°F - 41°F (0.5°C - 5°C)
- **Capacity**: 200 cubic feet
- **Power**: 208-230V, 60Hz, single phase
- **Refrigerant**: R-290 (Propane)
- **Dimensions**: 8ft x 10ft x 8ft (W x D x H)

### Daily Operation
1. **Startup**: Unit runs continuously. After power restoration, allow 4-6 hours to reach target temperature before loading product.
2. **Door Management**: Keep door closed when not in use. Door should not remain open longer than 2 minutes.
3. **Temperature Monitoring**: Check digital display twice per shift. Temperature should read between 33°F and 41°F.
4. **Loading**: Do not block air vents or evaporator fans. Maintain 3 inches clearance from walls. Do not exceed 80% capacity.
5. **Cleaning**: Wipe interior surfaces daily with food-safe sanitizer. Clean floor drain weekly.

### Temperature Alerts
| Condition | Temperature | Action Required |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| Normal | 33°F - 41°F | No action needed |
| Warning | 41°F - 45°F | Check door seal, reduce door openings, call maintenance if persists > 1 hour |
| Critical | Above 45°F | Move perishable items to backup cooler immediately. Call emergency maintenance. |
| Too Cold | Below 32°F | Check thermostat setting. Items may freeze — inspect produce and dairy. |

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Symptom | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Temperature rising | Door left open | Close door, allow 30 min to recover |
| Temperature rising | Dirty condenser coils | Schedule condenser cleaning (monthly maintenance) |
| Temperature rising | Refrigerant leak | Call certified technician immediately |
| Ice buildup on evaporator | Defrost cycle failure | Initiate manual defrost. If recurring, call technician. |
| Unusual noise | Fan motor bearing wear | Schedule fan motor replacement |
| Water on floor | Clogged drain line | Clear drain line with warm water flush |
| Unit not running | Power supply issue | Check breaker panel. Reset if tripped. |
| Unit not running | Compressor overload | Wait 15 minutes for auto-reset. If persists, call technician. |

### Safety Information
- Do not store chemicals or non-food items in the cooler
- Ensure emergency release handle on interior is functional (test monthly)
- Keep area around condenser unit clear of debris (minimum 12 inches clearance)
- Report any unusual odors (possible refrigerant leak) immediately
