# AnyCompany Freezer Unit Model FZ-500
## Product Manual & Operator Guide

### Product Overview
- **Model**: FZ-500 Commercial Reach-In Freezer
- **Target Temperature**: -5°F (-20.6°C)
- **Operating Range**: -10°F to 0°F (-23.3°C to -17.8°C)
- **Capacity**: 120 cubic feet
- **Power**: 208-230V, 60Hz, single phase
- **Refrigerant**: R-290 (Propane)
- **Dimensions**: 6ft x 3ft x 7ft (W x D x H)

### Daily Operation
1. **Startup**: After installation or power loss, allow 8-12 hours to reach target temperature before loading frozen product.
2. **Loading**: Stack items with airflow gaps. Do not overpack — maintain 75% max capacity for proper air circulation.
3. **Temperature Check**: Verify display reads between -10°F and 0°F each shift. Log readings.
4. **Door Discipline**: Open door only when necessary. Retrieve multiple items in one trip.
5. **Frost Management**: Light frost on interior walls is normal. Heavy ice buildup indicates a problem.

### Temperature Alerts
| Condition | Temperature | Action Required |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| Normal | -10°F to 0°F | No action needed |
| Warning | 0°F to 10°F | Check door seal, verify defrost cycle, call maintenance if persists > 2 hours |
| Critical | Above 10°F | Product safety risk. Move items to backup freezer. Call emergency maintenance. |
| Too Cold | Below -15°F | Check thermostat. Excessive cold wastes energy and can damage some products. |

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Symptom | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Temperature rising | Door gasket damaged | Inspect gasket for tears or gaps. Replace if damaged. |
| Temperature rising | Evaporator iced over | Initiate manual defrost cycle. If recurring, check defrost heater. |
| Temperature rising | Condenser coils dirty | Clean condenser coils (rear of unit) |
| Heavy ice on walls | Defrost timer malfunction | Call technician to replace defrost timer |
| Heavy ice on walls | Door not sealing | Replace door gasket |
| Compressor runs constantly | Low refrigerant | Call certified technician for leak check and recharge |
| Compressor runs constantly | Ambient temperature too high | Ensure kitchen ventilation is adequate |
| Frost on exterior | Door gasket leak | Replace door gasket |
| Alarm sounding | Temperature out of range | Check display. Follow temperature alert procedures above. |

### Defrost Cycle Information
- **Automatic Defrost**: Unit runs defrost cycle every 8 hours for 25 minutes
- **Manual Defrost**: Press and hold DEFROST button for 3 seconds. Unit will defrost for 30 minutes then resume cooling.
- **After Defrost**: Temperature may rise 5-10°F temporarily. This is normal. Unit will recover within 1 hour.
- **Important**: Never use sharp objects, heat guns, or hot water to remove ice. This can damage evaporator coils.

### Food Safety Guidelines
- Never refreeze thawed items
- Label all items with date frozen
- Follow FIFO (First In, First Out) rotation
- Discard any items that have been above 0°F for more than 4 hours
- Keep a backup thermometer inside the unit for verification
