# AnyCompany Beverage Cooler Model BC-3500
## Product Manual & Operator Guide

### Product Overview
- **Model**: BC-3500 Commercial Beverage Cooler
- **Target Temperature**: 35°F (1.7°C)
- **Operating Range**: 33°F - 38°F (0.5°C - 3.3°C)
- **Capacity**: 45 cubic feet, holds up to 600 cans/bottles
- **Power**: 115V, 60Hz, single phase
- **Refrigerant**: R-600a (Isobutane)
- **Dimensions**: 4ft x 2.5ft x 6.5ft (W x D x H)

### Daily Operation
1. **Startup**: Allow 2-3 hours to reach target temperature before stocking beverages.
2. **Stocking**: Load beverages from the back, rotating stock (FIFO). Do not block air circulation vents.
3. **Temperature Check**: Verify digital display reads 33°F-38°F at start of each shift.
4. **Glass Door Care**: Clean glass doors with glass cleaner daily. Check door closes fully and seals properly.
5. **Restocking**: Restock during low-traffic periods. Minimize door-open time.

### Temperature Alerts
| Condition | Temperature | Action Required |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| Normal | 33°F - 38°F | No action needed |
| Warning | 38°F - 42°F | Check door seal, ensure door closes fully |
| Critical | Above 42°F | Reduce stock, check condenser, call maintenance |
| Too Cold | Below 32°F | Beverages may freeze. Adjust thermostat up 2 degrees. |

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Symptom | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Beverages not cold enough | Overstocked | Remove excess stock, allow airflow |
| Beverages not cold enough | Dirty condenser | Clean condenser coils (underneath unit) |
| Condensation on glass | High humidity + frequent opening | Normal in humid conditions. Wipe as needed. |
| Door not closing | Misaligned hinges | Adjust hinge screws. If persistent, call maintenance. |
| Loud humming | Compressor working hard | Check ventilation around unit. Clean condenser. |
| Interior light out | Bulb burned out | Replace with LED bulb (model LED-BC35). Do NOT use incandescent. |
| Water pooling underneath | Drain pan full or clogged | Empty drain pan. Clear drain tube. |

### Energy Saving Tips
- Keep unit away from heat sources (ovens, direct sunlight)
- Ensure minimum 4 inches clearance on all sides for ventilation
- Stock beverages at room temperature or below — never hot
- Replace worn door gaskets promptly to prevent cold air loss
