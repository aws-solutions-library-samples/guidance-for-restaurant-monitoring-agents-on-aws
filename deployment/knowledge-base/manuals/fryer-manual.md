# AnyCompany Commercial Deep Fryer Model DF-375
## Product Manual & Operator Guide
## Covers: French Fry Station & Chicken Fryer

### Product Overview
- **Model**: DF-375 Commercial Deep Fryer
- **Target Temperature**: 375°F (190.5°C)
- **Operating Range**: 250°F - 400°F (121°C - 204°C)
- **Oil Capacity**: 50 lbs (twin vat, 25 lbs each)
- **Heat Source**: Natural gas or propane
- **BTU Rating**: 120,000 BTU/hr (60,000 per vat)
- **Dimensions**: 32in x 30in x 46in (W x D x H)
- **Applications**: French fries (Vat 1), Chicken (Vat 2) — dedicated to prevent flavor transfer

### Daily Operation

#### Startup Procedure
1. Verify oil level is between MIN and MAX lines on each vat
2. Check oil quality — discard if dark, foamy, or has off-odor
3. Open gas supply valve
4. Turn power switch to ON
5. Set each vat thermostat to 375°F
6. Allow 15-20 minutes for oil to reach temperature
7. Test with thermometer before first batch

#### Cooking Guidelines
| Product | Temperature | Cook Time | Basket Load |
|---------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| French Fries (frozen) | 375°F | 3-4 minutes | 2 lbs max per basket |
| French Fries (fresh cut) | 325°F then 375°F | Blanch 3 min, finish 2 min | 1.5 lbs max |
| Chicken Tenders | 375°F | 5-6 minutes | 1.5 lbs max |
| Chicken Wings | 375°F | 8-10 minutes | 2 lbs max |
| Chicken Sandwich Fillet | 375°F | 6-7 minutes | 4 pieces max |

#### Oil Management
- **Daily**: Check oil level and quality before first use
- **Filter oil**: Every 2-3 days or when quality degrades
- **Full oil change**: Every 7-10 days depending on volume
- **Oil quality test**: Use oil test strips (included in maintenance kit). Replace oil when reading exceeds 24% TPM.
- **Never mix**: Do not mix old and new oil. Do not mix different oil types.

#### Shutdown Procedure
1. Turn thermostats to OFF
2. Turn gas valve to OFF
3. Allow oil to cool below 150°F before any maintenance
4. Cover vats with lids to prevent contamination
5. Clean exterior surfaces
6. Wipe down surrounding area for grease splatter

### Temperature Alerts
| Condition | Temperature | Action Required |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| Normal | 365°F - 385°F | Optimal frying range |
| Low | Below 350°F | Oil recovering after large batch. Wait before next batch. |
| High | Above 400°F | Reduce thermostat. Check calibration. |
| Critical | Above 425°F | Turn off immediately. Oil approaching smoke point. Fire risk. |

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Symptom | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Oil not heating | Gas supply off | Check gas valve |
| Oil not heating | Thermocouple failure | Call technician to replace thermocouple |
| Oil heating slowly | Sediment buildup | Filter oil and clean vat interior |
| Oil foaming excessively | Oil degraded | Replace oil immediately |
| Oil foaming excessively | Water contamination | Ensure all items are dry before frying. Replace oil. |
| Food cooking unevenly | Oil temperature too low | Allow full recovery between batches |
| Food too dark | Oil temperature too high | Calibrate thermostat |
| Food too dark | Oil degraded | Replace oil |
| Grease fire | Oil exceeded flash point | Activate fire suppression system. NEVER use water. Use Class K extinguisher. |
| Gas smell | Gas leak | Turn off gas. Evacuate. Call gas company. |
| Pilot light won't stay lit | Thermocouple issue | Clean or replace thermocouple |

### Boil-Out Cleaning Procedure
1. Drain all oil when cool (below 150°F)
2. Fill vat with water to oil line
3. Add manufacturer-approved fryer cleaner (2 oz per gallon)
4. Heat water to 200°F and maintain for 20 minutes
5. Turn off heat and let cool
6. Drain water and rinse thoroughly with clean water
7. Dry completely before adding fresh oil (water causes dangerous splatter)

### Safety Information
- Keep Class K fire extinguisher within 6 feet of fryer
- Never move fryer with hot oil
- Always lower baskets slowly into hot oil to prevent splashing
- Ensure automatic fire suppression system is inspected semi-annually
- All operators must complete fryer safety training before use
- Never fry wet or frozen items with ice crystals — pat dry first
